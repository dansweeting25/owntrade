(07) FAQs

Common Questions.

Is the Website Really Free?

Yes, on Site + Ads. You pay £225 a month for me to run your Google Ads and host the site, and the website itself costs nothing. If you only want a website, it's £75 a month.

What If I Want to Leave?

You can leave any time. There's no contract, and your domain, Google Ads account and Google profile are in your name from the start.

Can I Stop the Ads but Keep the Site?

Yes. If it's before the 12 months are up, you move onto Site Only at £75 a month and the 12 months carry on.

How Much Should I Spend on Ads?

It depends on your trade and where you work. I'll suggest a starting budget on our first call. You pay Google directly. My fee covers up to £2,000 a month of ad spend, then it's 10%.

Who Owns the Google Ads Account?

You do. I manage it, but it's in your name from day one.

Do I Have to Write Anything?

No. A chat and a few photos of your work is all I need.
